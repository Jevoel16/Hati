Split shared bills and see who owes whom, live.

Hati keeps one record of your group’s expenses. Add a bill and every member’s balance updates on their screen right away, with no refresh.

Open the demo

How it works

  1. Create a group for your house, project or trip, and share its invite link.
  2. Add each expense with who paid. Split it equally, by exact amounts or by percentage.
  3. Hati works out each balance and the fewest payments that settle everyone.
  4. Record a payment once it is made, and the balances adjust for the whole group.

What you get

  • Balances and an activity feed that update live for everyone viewing the group.
  • Amounts kept to the centavo, so shares always add up to the bill.
  • A spending summary by category and by month.
  • Sign-in with Google or GitHub; group data is visible to its members only.
  • Works on a phone, in light or dark mode, and with the keyboard alone.

Questions

Does Hati move money?
No. Hati only keeps the record. You pay each other however you like, then record it.
What does “Hati” mean?
It is Filipino for “to split”.
Is it free?
Yes. Hati is a student project for ITE183, and its code is public on GitHub.
