Features

  • Live for everyone

    Balances and an activity feed update on every member’s screen, with no refresh.

  • Exact to the centavo

    Shares always add up to the bill, however you split it.

  • Payment QRs

    Your GCash, Maya and bank InstaPay QRs. The payer picks one and gets the exact amount owed filled in.

  • Fewest payments

    Hati settles the whole group with as few transfers as possible.

  • Spending summary

    See what the group spent by category and by month.

  • Private groups

    Sign in with Google or GitHub; group data is visible to its members only.

  • Anywhere

    Works on a phone or a desktop, in light or dark mode, and with the keyboard alone.
