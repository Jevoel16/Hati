How it works

  1. Create a group

    Make one for your house, project or trip, and share its invite link or QR code.

  2. Add each expense

    Say who paid, then split it equally, by exact amounts or by percentage.

  3. See who owes whom

    Hati works out each balance and the fewest payments that settle everyone.

  4. Settle up

    Pick one of the payee’s InstaPay QRs, which shows the exact amount, then record the payment and the balances adjust for the whole group.
