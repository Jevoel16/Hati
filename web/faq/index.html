Questions

Does Hati move money?
No. Hati only keeps the record. You pay each other however you like, then record it.
Can I pay with GCash, Maya or my bank?
Yes. Add your InstaPay QR on your account page, and the people who owe you get it with the exact amount filled in, ready to scan or share in the group chat.
What does “Hati” mean?
It is Filipino for “to split”.
Is it free?
Yes. Hati is a student project for ITE183, and its code is public on GitHub.
